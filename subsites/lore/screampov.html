MM_Scream's POV of Season 2


I was checking my phone when I heard that a new Minecraft server was starting. I had previously been playing on a server with a bunch of dumbasses who would quit whenever they got killed, so I decided to join the new server and see how it was. I started grinding for loot and discovered several different mods that I didn't even know were installed. At first, I had a hard time finding a teammate, but eventually Jelly joined the VC. I asked him to team with me, and he agreed. Unfortunately, he wasn't able to play for a few days, so I had to grind on my own.

I eventually found a good spot for a base and started setting up. It was ugly at first—nothing more than a trading hall with some storage—but Jelly eventually helped me grind trades, and we found a few that we liked. Before we bought any of them, though, I received a request from Lego to trade for my Fire Gauntlet. I TPA'd to him and was met with an obsidian hole surrounded by armor stands. Dripstone fell, and I died. I returned to my base with Jelly, gathered some spare loot, and hunted them down, wearing invisibility cloaks, of course. We tracked them through the Nether and eventually followed them to their base on the Nether roof. We lay low and waited for them to log off, then raided the base.

The next day, we got our enchants set up and started trying to obtain Netherite. It went well, and we were eventually able to get full Netherite armor. Soon after, we discovered a group called the Coalition, led by Lego and Highlighter. Ironically, these were the same people who had trapped me at the beginning of the server, and they eventually became some of our closest allies. The Coalition's goal was to kill Caz. We spent weeks forming a plan, and eventually, on one fateful day when everyone was online, we carried it out. Unfortunately, it was not successful.

Hidden inside the stronghold were me, Highlighter, Jelly, Lew, Serpent, and a few others. Lego TPA'd Caz to us, and we immediately jumped him. Caz TPA'd to Jace, and the two of them came back on foot, not quite knowing what they were getting themselves into. Many casualties happened that day, including Jace, Jelly, and Serpent. Jelly and I were able to recover fairly easily, but it was much harder for Serpent.

Then came the hacking period. I heard that Aaron was using Meteor, so I decided I would use it too. I elytra-boosted myself across countless blocks, as many as my heart desired. At one point, I even used the elytra boost to narrowly escape a jump by Caz and Aaron.

One night, I was thinking about how much I wanted power—as much of it as possible—so I called Lego and told him my plan. I was going to make a command block that gave players "health indicators," and I was going to have Jace place it, which would supposedly lead to me becoming an operator. I kept thinking about it until I came up with an almost foolproof plan: a backdoor.

I spent weeks learning how to code it. I was a complete beginner with zero experience, but eventually, I managed to get it working on my test server. Unfortunately, it was for the wrong Fabric version for Jace's server. I revised it many times, repeatedly claiming that it was "from a friend." Eventually, it worked. I put the code in and... I was an operator.

Caz and I quickly spawned in our items, and I kept most of them. I called Lego and asked him to help me make a stash. We worked on it together, putting every single item into the stash. We even included a void hole for emergencies. The stash had a few close calls. One involved an Ender Dragon that narrowly missed the items. The other was a creeper—not a hacked creeper, just a completely normal one. It somehow destroyed every single item in the stash. Lego's game crashed because of the sheer number of items on the ground, and when he joined back, everything was gone. Luckily, I still had plenty of items in my Ender Chest.

Later that night, I got a call from Jace and Aaron. They had discovered my backdoor. Aaron was testing it while Jace restarted the server to remove my mod and operator permissions, but Aaron had perfect, completely accidental timing. He activated the backdoor and made me an operator again just before the server restarted.

Afterward, Caz, Jelly, and I were sentenced to a duel. Jelly and Caz were banned for one day, while I was banned for two. Once I returned to the server, I began working on my second stash—a private, secure stash that contained even more items than the first one.

One night, I found Lew's base and wanted to show him some of the items I had obtained. One of them was a creeper egg. I didn't really know what it did, so I placed it down. It proceeded to blow up his entire base. We repaid him with full Netherite sets.

After that, I had something of a revelation. I talked to Lego about the items and eventually decided that I should stop using them. Instead, I started spending my time building impressive things. My main project was a lighthouse at spawn. I became peaceful with everyone, and for a while, we all lived happily.

That peace eventually ended when I received a DM from Jace telling me that it was my last warning: if I continued using Meteor, I would be permanently banned. I wasn't willing to give it up, so I called a buddy who had been whitelisted at the very beginning of the server but had never actually played. I figured mutually assured destruction was the best way to handle the situation. I gave him the coordinates to a shulker containing a server-crashing spawner, along with more OP items. Thankfully, I never had to use it.

Eventually, I lost all of my real stuff. I died. I won't disclose how. My items despawned, and after that, I quit the server entirely.
